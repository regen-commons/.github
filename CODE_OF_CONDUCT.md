# Regen Commons code of conduct

We want Regen Commons to be a welcoming place to contribute, learn, and disagree with care. These expectations apply in our GitHub spaces and when representing Regen Commons in community activities.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect across backgrounds, identities, cultures, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and work without attacking people. Make room for questions, different perspectives, and constructive disagreement.
- Credit contributions and sources. Be honest about evidence, uncertainty, mistakes, and conflicts of interest.
- Respect consent and privacy. Ask before sharing someone else's personal information, private conversations, or community knowledge.
- Take responsibility for the effects of your work. Listen to people affected by it and help repair harm when it occurs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, persistent unwanted contact, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Neither are deliberate misrepresentation of contributions or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting a concern

Email [regencommons@gmail.com](mailto:regencommons@gmail.com) to ask for a private conversation with a steward, or contact a steward you trust directly. The [public stewardship updates](https://hub.regencoordination.xyz/c/regen-commons/33) are the place to find council information. If the concern involves someone who may receive the shared email, contact another steward directly.

Share only the detail needed to start the conversation. Reports should be handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to assess the concern and respond. Do not post sensitive incident details in a public GitHub issue.

## Responding to harm

Stewards and repository maintainers may ask for a change in behavior, remove harmful content, or restrict participation in the spaces they manage. Responses should reflect the severity and pattern of the behavior, protect affected people, and allow room for repair where appropriate. A person involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome.
