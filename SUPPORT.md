# Getting help

- **A problem with a tool or repository:** Read its README and search existing issues. Open an issue in that repository with steps to reproduce, the expected result, and your environment. Remove personal data and secrets from screenshots and logs.
- **An idea, resource, or community question:** Visit the [Regen Commons forum](https://hub.regencoordination.xyz/c/regen-commons/33).
- **Membership, partnerships, or finding the right project:** Visit [regencommons.com](https://regencommons.com/) or email [regencommons@gmail.com](mailto:regencommons@gmail.com).
- **A security vulnerability:** Follow the [security guidance](https://github.com/regen-commons/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) before sharing details.
- **A conduct concern:** Use the reporting route in the [code of conduct](https://github.com/regen-commons/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Repository-specific support instructions take precedence for that project. Response times depend on the people maintaining it; there is no organization-wide support service level.
