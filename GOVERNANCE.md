# Stewardship and decisions

Regen Commons brings people and organizations together to care for shared resources and coordinate regenerative work. Its stewardship council convenes the wider community work. The [community forum](https://hub.regencoordination.xyz/c/regen-commons/33) carries public updates, proposals, and discussions, including the [Q4 2026 stewardship update](https://hub.regencoordination.xyz/t/update-from-the-stewardship-council-on-regen-commons-q4-2026/474).

This page explains how GitHub contributions fit into that work. It is not a constitution or a replacement for community agreements.

## Repository decisions

Use issues to describe problems, propose changes, and record scope. Use pull requests to review changes to code and documentation. Follow the receiving project's contribution guidance and review requirements; maintainers manage changes within that repository.

When a proposed change affects membership, shared assets, licensing, funding, or a commitment to another organization, bring it to the responsible stewards and community process. Record the resulting decision and its scope before presenting it as agreed. Merging a pull request does not, by itself, approve those commitments.

## Community agreements and work in progress

The Commons is developing ways to share knowledge and other assets with consent, attribution, and reciprocity, including work on the Regen Commons Unified License (RCUL). Use published agreements and the license attached to a specific asset to determine its terms. A discussion, draft, or meeting summary should remain identified as such.

The Commons' institutional home and relationships with partner initiatives are evolving. For current membership and stewardship questions, use the forum or [regencommons@gmail.com](mailto:regencommons@gmail.com).

Regen Coordination and the Regen Knowledge Commons are related efforts with their own projects and decisions. Collaboration or a link from this organization does not transfer their governance or ownership to Regen Commons.
