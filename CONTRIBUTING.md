# Contributing to Regen Commons

Contributions can be code, documentation, research, design, testing, translation, or community knowledge. A useful correction or a well-described problem is a good place to start.

## Find a place to contribute

1. Browse our [repositories](https://github.com/orgs/regen-commons/repositories) and read the relevant project's README, contribution guidance, and license.
2. Look for an existing issue before opening a new one. Describe the need, who it affects, and what a useful outcome would look like. Discuss substantial changes with the maintainers before beginning.
3. Use the [Regen Commons forum](https://hub.regencoordination.xyz/c/regen-commons/33) for community ideas, partnerships, and questions that span projects. For membership or help finding a starting point, email [regencommons@gmail.com](mailto:regencommons@gmail.com).

## Prepare your contribution

- Keep the change focused and explain why it helps. Follow the project's own setup, technical choices, and review process.
- For software, run the relevant checks and test the behavior you changed. Report anything you could not verify.
- For research and documentation, cite sources, check links, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and mark proposals or uncertain claims clearly.
- Share only material you have permission to publish. Respect attribution, licensing, community consent, and restrictions on cultural or place-based knowledge. Keep private meeting notes, personal data, and credentials out of public issues and commits.
- If you use AI tools, review the result yourself. Verify claims, sources, and code before submitting; follow any project-specific disclosure requirements.

## Submit and review

Open a pull request with a short explanation, the related issue if there is one, and evidence of your checks. A draft is welcome while work is in progress. For work that does not fit a pull request, use the relevant issue to agree on a format with maintainers.

Maintainers review the contribution and may ask for changes. A merged contribution records a project change; it does not by itself grant membership, approve funding, or ratify a community agreement. See [Stewardship and decisions](https://github.com/regen-commons/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).

## Paid work and licenses

If work is funded, agree on scope, reviewer, budget, payment terms, and completion criteria with the responsible project before starting. An issue or pull request alone is not a promise of payment.

Check the receiving repository's license before contributing. Preserve existing copyright notices and attribution. Work on the Regen Commons Unified License does not automatically change the license of any repository or contributed asset.

Please follow our [code of conduct](https://github.com/regen-commons/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Report vulnerabilities through the [security guidance](https://github.com/regen-commons/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
